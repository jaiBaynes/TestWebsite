*Myth 15.62: Lernia Lake and the Beehive

The Microcosms of Melissa and Hydra dragged their enemies into a world completely under their control.
Mel trapped her opponent in a bizarre real of rigid metal walls, overflowing with electricity. The structure was expansive yet crammed: strange, thin and sturdy hexagonal hallways made up this labyrinthian realm. It was like a giant metallic factory, contorted to look like a structure of nature.
Prodotes had the power of Zeus on his side, but even he screamed in agony when shocked by the high voltage floor of this bizarre realm! He had to fly just to stay alive. However, in his rush to escape the floor he hit the wall and was shocked again! This awful place had too much electricity for even Zeus’ protection to fend off. This was Mel’s Microcosm: Beehive.
“Welcome to the Beehive, Prodotes.” Mel said as she casually walked across the electrified floor. Rather than harming her, it was charging her up. The tide of battle had shifted dramatically.
Likewise, Hydra stood alone with Asterion in a vast swamp. It was surprisingly understated, just a normal looking swamp. Yet at the heart of the swamp was a great mysterious and opaque lake. The water’s supernatural surface tension allowed the two combatants to stand on the liquid’s surface without even needing to fly. This was Hydra’s Microcosm: Lerna Lake.
“There we go. Just the two of us, now we can fight without distractions.” Hydra smirked.
“Yes, we can in my Microcosm!” Asterion shouted as he Powered up, ready to displace Hydra’s Microcosm with his own. But nothing happened. The god was very proud of his unique Microcosm. A shame it wasn’t strong enough to overtake Hydra’s. Asterion began to panic. He… hadn’t expected this. How did Hydra have enough Power to keep this maintained and defy being overtaken in a Microcosm clash!?
“Now you get the predicament you’re in.” the water snake said as he marched across the lake’s surface towards his hated adversary.
Asterion instinctively tried to flee, but out of the lake on which he stood came all sorts of awful sea monsters. The great beasts surrounded Asterion, ready to maul him to death. This was just like… just like with Poseidon.
“Asterion, you are a man so wicked that you had to be removed from your post by Neptune himself. It’s unfortunate to see that though your reputation was rehabilitated, you haven’t been.” Hydra noted.
Asterion froze as it all came back to him. He was a god… but the second he defied a higher power… the second he went up against a Solaris he could do nothing. In a nearly identical scenario, Asterion found himself on an endless sea, surrounded by a veritable army of otherworldly sea monsters! That repressed nightmare that kept him up at night was coming back.
“Damn Neptune and damn you! I have Zeus on my side! Don’t go talking as if you’ve won!” Asterion screamed, refusing to be defeated now as he was then.
“Haven’t I?” Hydra laughed. Asterion froze, remembering his dire situation. He again stood outmatched: surrounded by monsters from the demonic lake, with the eyes of many more creatures stalking him from the swampy shadows.
The beasts were not as numerous or powerful as Neptune’s to be sure… but unfortunately for Asterion, Hydra’s mercy too paled in comparison to the god of the seas.
Mel likewise looked up at Prodotes. He was wounded and scared of even touching the walls. Then he heard them, the awful buzzing noises of a swarm of giant electric bees! Prodotes panicked. They were flying at him from one side while a charged-up Mel approached with her massive hammer on the other side.
He began clashing his weapons together using the shockwave to try and stun all enemies. Mel’s powered up electric field completely blocked the shockwave. All it seemed to do was anger her bee constructs.
Prodotes panicked as the closer they got the more he could see they were giant bee monsters! They began spitting some strange yellow substance at him. He dodged a few but a stray shot hit is arm. The bizarre honey of sorts began expanding and growing heavier and heavier.
As he was weighed down, he was getting hit by more and more until his entire body was coated in the honey and weighed down to the ground!
He screamed in agony as the honey conducted the electricity and hurt him even more than when his bare skin touched the electrified floor! Things weren’t looking good for him.
In his fight with Hydra, Asterion likewise began to panic but suddenly had an idea.
“Stay back! Even if it is remotely, I can execute all my minions from in here! One more step and I can kill them all!”
“Really?” Hydra asked.
“Yes, you care about those weaklings! Just think about all the awful things I will do to them if you don’t listen!” Asterion cackled.
“Really? Are you thinking about it?” Hydra asked.
“Oh yes. I can see it now: every I can picture killing them in every way a soft-hearted fool like you fears! And if you don’t do as I say, those thoughts will be reality!” Asterion grinned.
“Well, something interesting I want to know… you don’t care about them, but you at least remember your minions?” Hydra asked.
“Yeah, so?” Asterion demanded.
“Remember that guy with that one ability Talio?” Hydra asked.
“What about him?” Asterion demanded.
“My Microcosm has this nifty little effect: while within it, I can recreate any ability that would’ve killed me… if I wasn’t a monster.” Hydra explained as his eyes glowed like his father’s.
Asterion froze in utter despair as he heard that.
“Yeah. All those awful things you want to do to those people you’ve enslaved… I can’t think of a more fitting end than for me to grant them a bit of… retaliation.” Hydra grinned.
Asterion screamed before his body erupted into a cacophony of gore as simultaneously each and every violent torture that he wanted to inflict on others was bounced back onto him. Asterion was killed by his own wickedness in a fitting end.
At the same time, Mel stood over the dying Prodotes as her bees readied to finish him.
“Please Mel, stop! Your father wouldn’t want this…” Prodotes pleaded for his life.
“You’re right.” Melissa the daughter of Melisseus admitted. Prodotes breathed a sigh of relief. Of course, foolishness and weakness ran in the family!
“My father was a kind man who hated violence. He wouldn’t do this or want others to do it on his behalf.” Melissa agreed.
Prodotes did his best to conceal a wicked grin. While she was monologuing, he was storing up strength… ready to sneak attack and kill her.
“My father was a good and merciful man.” Mel smiled fondly. Prodotes prepared to make his move as he saw her distraction.
“But I’m not my father.” Mel warned as a surge of blood lusted lightning sparked from her! Prodotes at once abandoned any hope of ever overcoming the mighty demigoddess.
“You’re the one who said to move past his naivety. I have.” Mel coldly replied.
Prodotes’ eyes widened in shock and fear before the queen bee’s swarm descended upon him and tore him limb from limb. As Prodotes screamed and cried at his dismemberment, the last thing he saw was Mel raising his hammer to finish the job… before squashing him like a bug.
