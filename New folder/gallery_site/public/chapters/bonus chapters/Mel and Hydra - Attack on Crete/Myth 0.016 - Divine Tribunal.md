*Myth 15.63: Divine Tribunal

Hydra and Mel rushed towards Minos. They both sensed large Powers nearby but the thick Adamant walls and the mysterious Solaris Power coursing through the Palace of Knossos made it hard to tell who it was they were heading towards. But after killing his strongest guys in the last battle… that had to be all the minions he had, right?
They bolted ahead, kicking down wall after wall that Minos threw up to try and stop them. Then they both came crashing through opposite walls and slammed into each other.
“Ow.” Both said while holding the points of collision and groaning.
“Damnit Hydra! Watch where you’re going.” Mel got back to her feet.
“Glad to see you too Mel.” Hydra chuckled as he stood up as well.
“So. With any luck… the only remaining major source of Power… will be Minos.” Mel made a fist.
“Which is exactly why he’s probably hiding his Power to sneak away. I can’t sense any other sources.” Hydra pointed out.
Sure enough, they turned just a few more corners and kicked down the door to the saferoom where Minos and his guests were originally hiding. The room was completely empty.
“Damn it, you’re right! We killed his toughest guys so that coward will show his true colors.” Mel slammed the empty throne in anger. But then she realized something.
“But if he’s left his throne… he doesn’t have the controls to manipulate the Palace anymore.” Mel realized as she saw the controls right there.
“That’s right! Mel, where is the nearest secret escape?” Hydra cheered.
Minos and his guests made a mad dash to the escape route they should’ve gone to a long time ago. Minos opened the door, ecstatic to finally be home free. However, to the nobles’ horror they found themselves face to face with the safe room they fled from… and Mel and Hydra!
“Oh…” a terrified Minos said.
“These palace shifting controls are pretty nifty. Got a nice ease of use design so even a child could use it. Figures since you figured out how to work it.” Hydra said while lounging in Minos’ throne.
“What do you want? Do you want money?” Minos rolled his eyes.
“It’s not about the money… it’s about sending a message.” Hydra grinned.
The nobles were terrified.
“You like that one? I’ve been practicing that for one of my villain monologues. When I’ve got to act for the public there’s a character I’ve got to play. Can’t be all goofy and fun when I’m trying to get your fellow nobles in Graia to piss their perizomas.  But y’know, nobody who sees me here today will live to tell the tale. And besides… this is a much more personal mission.” Hydra said.
All eyes turned to Mel who was itching for the chance to start hammering Minos. All the nobles grew even more terrified. They loved watching violence, but the thought of even a fraction of that coming back to them…
Minos, Rhadamanthus and Sarpedon stood no chance against Mel or Hydra’s teamwork alone. The three rivalrous brothers, looked to each other in mutual understanding. Minos, Rhadamanthus and Sarpedon, three brothers formerly quarreling and at each others’ throats were united by their class solidarity against this revolution. That was the only way for one class to successfully defeat the other after all.
With that worldly revelation, the three brothers also received a Divine one. Yes, in that moment, inexplicable and illogically, in defiance of what was expected or even fair they received a Deus ex Machina: they all received an Epiphany.
These who upheld the current system of power were now bailed out by the system of Power! Their growth as people in parallel featured a dazzling growth in might. The blaze around them was so overwhelming that even Hydra and Mel struggled to remain standing in the face of it.
“What in the Kosmos is going on!?” Mel demanded at this inexplicable sight.
“Not good!” Hydra alone recognized the once in a lifetime phenomenon they were witnessing three times at once.
At once he fired his flaming eyes at them, but the intense Power of the three kings repelled it!
“They’re receiving an Epiphany! Their strength is being heightened and they’ll each be granted an annoying special ability like all those minions we put down!” Hydra shouted as he continued attacking in vain.
Mel gasped. This was an Epiphany? Why? Why was fate itself working against them and empowering these fools? She’d trained for years for this moment- she’d risked her life to get the weapons and armor required for this raid. And now, these lazy layabouts were getting a get out of jail free card from some magic BS out of nowhere!?
“Bullshit!” Mel screamed and hurled her hammer at them. It too was repelled by the unnatural phenomenon her targets were caught up in.
As the light died down, Mel and Hydra braced themselves. Things just got a lot harder. The three kings simultaneously opened their eyes and grinned confidently. They knew just what to do.
Their eyes were opened by this Revelation and each overflowed with incredible divine Power.
“We, the supreme judges chosen by Zeus shall be your Judge, Jury and Executioner!” they declared as all their Powers burst forth and the three activate their new joint Microcosm: the Divine Tribunal!
A massive court room appears with Mel and Hydra as defendants before the three kings of Crete as judges. The other nobles just watched as audience members: in the court of kings, no other opinion needed to be considered.
“Great… got enough juice left for this?” Hydra asked through a nervous laugh.
“Whatever it takes to kill them!” Mel cried as she tried to dispel their Microcosm with her own.
However, it didn’t work! Hydra did the same to similar effect. The kings just laughed at their tired foes.
“You utter fools! You cannot displace this Microcosm, because it is in fact 3 overlapping Microcosms powered by three divine Swords of Damocles!” Minos, Rhadamanthus and Sarpedon spoke in unison.
Each received a perfect blueprint to build this ultimate technique as a perfect counter to their opponent. Without training a day in their lives, they had all the skill of a Power master just handed to them on a silver platter. Their own thoughts and memories were recontextualized and imbued with the exact kind of BS power needed to get a hero out of a narrative corner.
The brothers, as fully adult men, finally grasped the meaning of teamwork and so their internal growth shaped this external manifestation. Nothing brought the feuding upper class together quite like any threat to their power. All that energy they spent feuding and harming innocents in the crossfire was squarely focussed on Hydra and Mel now.
“Alright. We’ll just do this another way.” Hydra smiled as his flaming eyes prepared to fire.
Then the flames went out. Mel and Hydra were shocked.
“Villains, there shall be order in the court! Only vulgarians like you resort to violence to settle disputes. We shall settle this in the court of law!” the three brothers spoke as one.
“In your crooked, biased court!? How is that fair?” Mel roared.
“There shall be no violence in our courtroom. That is our rule and all must abide by it.” The brothers declared.
“Yeah? But I’m pretty sure you intend on killing us somehow at the end of this trial.” Hydra called them out.
“Of course.” They replied.
“Then you’re full of it! That is violence, I call an objection or whatever it’s called!” Hydra cried.
“Overruled.” Minos smirked.
“Besides, it is not violence. It is just the impartial will of justice. The law is not violent: it dispels violence, villains.” Sarpedon added.
“In this trial, you shall be tried, found guilty and executed. That is all.” Rhadamanthus concluded.
Mel and Hydra looked at each other nervously. It seemed like the judges had already made their minds up. Naturally, trapped in a broken legal system, they had no choice but to fight for their freedom.
Mel leapt towards the judges with a punch ready, but no Power came to attack. But how!? She used Power to jump this far. Was the rule of no violence really that absolute? Before Mel could make another move, she was tackled to the ground by magically constructed Bailiffs. They restrained her aggressively and she growled in pain as Hydra heard awful snapping. What a nonviolent justice system they had.
Mel was forced back to her defendant’s seat as Hydra’s blood boiled. However, the second he tried using his Power for violent purposes, he would lose it and instead be victimized by the violent arm of the kings’ justice. They couldn’t dispel the Microcosm and they couldn’t fight in it. What could they do?
“Mel, are you alright?” Hydra pleaded.
“I’m fine… this is nothing compared to what I’m going to do to them.” Mel explained.
“As everyone in this court room was just witness to, you just committed attempted murder. For that crime, Melissa daughter of Melisseus, you are sentenced to death!” the Judges convicted her instantly.
