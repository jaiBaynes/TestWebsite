*Myth 15.6:  Mel vs Prodotes

Mel’s divine lightning carved up the hallway of their confrontation but before it could strike Prodotes, lightning of his own sparked out and protected him. He too was overflowing with the divine lightning of Zeus.
“That’s it? You betrayed us for some fancy spark powers from that tyrant!?” Mel demanded.
“That tyrant… is your true father.” Prodotes declared.
“Shut up!!!” Mel screamed, firing off sparks again. They did nothing to Prodotes whose equal powers cancelled it out.
“How dare you even wear the armor of the Kuretes. You sold Crete out to Graia, you even let them parade the name around for some band of contract killers!” Mel pointed her hammer at him furiously.
“I made the right decision. Your father… that man Melisseus was being idealistic and childish. He refused to grow up and see the truth. I did. That’s why he’s dead and I still live, better off than ever before. Do not fall prey to his childish delusions too.” Prodotes declared as he raised his own Power to match Mel’s.
The furious demigoddess leapt forward and swung her hammer, clashing with Prodotes’ sword. As their weapons collided, an awful shockwave emanated from Prodotes’ and blasted Mel! That awful noise dulled her senses and damaged her body.
Undeterred, she swung again, this time hitting his shield. Prodotes was sent flying even though he blocked the attack. Yet the same awful ringing from his shield being stuck left Mel just as worse for wear.
He still had it: the special technique of the Kuretes given to them by their Goddess Rhea. It had served them well in protecting her champion Zeus and still did. Yes, this was all her fault… Rhea or rather Terra as most people called her now had foolishly chosen to back that tyrant. All this was her fault. Mel’s anger powered her up as she prepared to strike again, consequences be damned!
The furious warrior heaved around her mallet of equal weight to her anger. Blow after blow, hefty smash after smash she demolished all in her path as she battered her opponent all about! With great physical strength, Mel’s relentless attack carved a path of destruction through her former home.
The warrioress though filled with wrath still moved with the grace and lightning precision of the Cretan warriors. Perfectly able to read her opponent’s movements she never failed to strike with the most fortuitous and brutal angle. Prodotes, who had once been tasked with teaching her these techniques struggled to keep up with Melisseus’ peer in skill!
Old Prodotes struggled to remain standing however, he grinned as he saw the attacks were taking a toll on Mel as well. The amplified blowback from each strike was wreaking havoc on Mel. After smashing her opponent across the room yet again, Mel prepared to give chase but stumbled clumsily. Looking down, she saw that she was bleeding… perhaps more than her opponent was. All her adrenaline and hate distracted her from just how much damage she had taken in the clashes.
“You don’t seem as soft as your father… but your weakness is that your own anger will destroy you.” Prodotes declared.
“Shut up!!!” Mel roared while hurling her hammer.
Prodotes smiled as the massive weapon came hurdling towards him. He could probably block it with his sword and shield, but why bother. In her anger, Mel had just thrown away her one weapon! Prodotes dodged past the hammer as he charged towards Mel at full speed. Now, he would punish that wrathful outburst. Now he would kill her!
Prodotes with Zeus’ speed swung his sword to slay the princess of Crete. Mel simply coated her fist in lightning and held it out, stopping Prodotes’ attack in its tracks. But how!? Hydra was an idiot and nerd… but his anachronistic knowledge of physics had finally proven useful to Mel. She was no scientist but one of those oaf’s ramblings had come into play: opposites attract but like charges repel. Her lightning guard and Prodotes’ lightning attack could not touch! Magnets: that’s how they worked.
While the bronze age warrior was frozen in mystification at this first-grade science display, Mel put her other hand out, uncovered in lightning. Prodotes prepared to strike it, excited that this time his strike was not stopped. But he froze. Why was Mel so willing to offer up her hand. With his divinely enchanted weapon, he would inflict a wound that drained her of great Power and would be a pain to heal. What would be worth sacrificing a whole arm for.
Then Prodotes heard it: a clap of thunder behind him. He turned around and saw it: Mylopetra, Mel’s hammer racing towards Mel’s hand and by extension him! The hand was a diversion, he would claim her arm but if that thing hit him from behind, she would claim his life!
Prodotes had no choice but to leap away as Mel caught her hammer and prepared to throw it again! This wasn’t good. Her lightning couldn’t harm him, but that gargantuan thing bouncing around would be an issue. Prodotes had no other choice.
Using the signature technique of the Kuretes, Prodotes smashed his sword and shield together, firing off a shockwave that blasted Mel off her feet. All the kinetic energy they stored could be bounced back at enemies. It was the ability of that accursed goddess who handed out such gifts so cavalierly. In a sense Mel was fighting Prodotes, her own blowback, Zeus and that goddess all at once.
Prodotes was just fighting Mel… but he was barely hanging on. For that matter neither was the Palace of Knossos. Entire rooms and wings of the palace were being obliterated in massive thunderous smashes and explosions of lightning. The epic clash of Mel’s thunderhead attacks and the violent gong-like shockwaves released every time Prodotes was smashed around made the palace host to the Kosmos’ most violent game of crocket.
Minos for his part was trying at first to shift the palace around to aid Prodotes. However, he quickly became more concerned with shifting whatever room the battle was currently taking place in to be as far away from him as possible. Thunderbolt Mel was so fast that Minos’ surveillance system was barely able to keep track of her. At times it would lose her completely!
All the previous battles, all those warmups were supposed to be what wore Mel down, but she wasn’t trying… until now. Every full power swing she hit Prodotes with was enough to kill all her other opponents combined. Every second she stayed at max Power output; she was putting out more Power than her previous battles altogether. She REALLY wanted to kill Prodotes.
After flying through his fiftieth brick wall, Prodotes lay in rubble he realized was destroyed from the last time Mel smashed him through here. He looked to his shield which rang like a bell. If it blocked another hit from Mel’s hammer it would shatter. Prodotes had to swap and use his sword for a while. His weapons were meant to shatter whatever attacked them, but Mel’s hammer was sturdier and now threatened to break them.
Prodotes stood up and clashed his sword with Mel’s hammer. The blade chipped. Like lightning she immediately swung again. Now Prodotes had to swap to his shield and give his sword time to stop vibrating. Back and forth over and over again, he had to keep swapping sword and shield as Mel just kept swinging like a madwoman! Hephaestus’ work was top tier: the hammer he made for Mel was in pristine shape while Prodotes’ were the ones threatening to go to pieces.
Conversely… Prodotes was still unharmed because with enchantment from both Zeus and Terra his weapons and armor could absorb the brunt of Mel’s attacks. Meanwhile, Mel’s body was tearing itself apart more than ever as each deflection shockwave hit her faster than her demigod body could regenerate. Prodotes smiled. Just a bit longer.
Yes, the second Mel broke his sword and shield, she would splatter him with her hammer. However, if they lasted longer than she did… Prodotes won. Her own anger would be her destruction! Prodotes smashed his sword and shield both into Mel’s hammer swing. The incredible shockwave they released made Mel stagger backwards. She vomited up blood. Even pure adrenaline wasn’t enough to keep the wounded warrior for much longer. Prodotes could practically taste victory as Mel sank to her knees, to catch her breath.
He prepared to move in and finish her. Then she stood back up.
“How annoying.” Mel grumbled as she stood back up, shockingly still ready to continue fighting.
