*Myth 15.52: The Palace of Knossos

The Palace of Knossos was an ancient and sacred Cretan site. Originally built as an Olympian military outpost during the great Titan War, it became sacred due to its divine associations. Powered by Zeus itself, this sacred place was retrofitted to direct the thunder power towards domestic usages such as water and waste management rather than weaponry.
In Melisseus’ day, the central base was expanded to contain a vast and open aired royal residence and courthouse. The king of Crete, elected to life was king and judge both who made sure his palace, though guarded well still maintained the appearance and atmosphere of accessibility and openness. Countless great marble rooms were connected by long, decorated hallways, all supported by beautiful crimson pillars.
The walls had iconography of everyday life, of bounty and love. Accompanying such images were those of heroism and triumph: showing the palace’s uses and successes in war with the enemy Titans. Lastly divine iconography graced key sections of the palace, paying homage to Crete’s patron gods: Rhea and her champion Zeus. Their symbols, sacred animals and likenesses were among the most immaculate and incredible art pieces of the Palace. Bulls, dolphins and octopi were the main sacred animals, for which each wing of the palace corresponded.
While the inner walls hosted such things, the outer walls had a very different kind of appeal to them. From almost anywhere in the palace, residents could soak in the pristine Cretan landscape. All Cretans living around or entering the people’s palace soaked in its beauty with pride.
However, under Minos this beauty was kept for himself. Erecting Great walls around his captured estate, he enclosed what was once open and inviting. Its wealth and beauty were his and his alone. Around the warm and inviting villa were fortifications and intimidating garrisons. Rather than anyone being allowed in under watch of the king’s guards, now only those cordially invited by his sacred majesty could set foot in its halls.
It was primarily for this reason that Minos needed the camera system installed: none but the top floors of the palace could see over the hideous fortifications and into the great landscape around it. The open aired palace was purposeless when there was nothing to see.
Nonetheless, Mel and Hydra tore through these walls and forts without much effort. The army of Minos gave chase, desperate to keep the invaders from the sacrosanct palace itself. The daughter of Crete and the beast of Lerna still made their way into the palace as alarms blared. The opened windows were all closed with thick Adamant blast doors. They were now stuck inside the dim and mysterious structure. When under attack, the palace could revert to its wartime nature.
“The Palace of Knossos. Always wanted to visit. Got time to give me a tour, princess?” the charming serpent grinned while sprinting through the palace halls.
“Just follow me and you won’t die.” Mel explained while sprinting past him despite the giant hammer that she was carrying around.
“Right, but aren’t they on the top floor? We could just fly up there and kill them, couldn’t we?” Hydra asked.
“The alarm has been rung and the castle has discretely transformed into siege mode. That room is now a decoy trap that is rigged to explode and kill everyone inside the second they enter.” Mel explained.
“Oh. Good to know.” Hydra chuckled.
“Like I said, follow me and you won’t die.” Mel explained.
“Speaking of the alarm being sprung, I know our cover is kind of shot, but did you have to go and tell everyone we were here to assassinate Minos?” her villainous co-conspirator asked.
“I had to get their attention off the innocent people outside. It worked, didn’t it?” Mel asked as she looked back at the literal army chasing them.
“We should be way faster than them, how are they so close behind?” Hydra looked back in disbelief as he continued running far faster than most gods could even keep up with.
“Like I said, the palace is in siege mode. To protect against invaders, it is capable of shifting around its various components. Even if we got ten rooms away from them, Minos could shift them to be right behind us.” Mel said.
The villain looked back and saw it in action. The Palace of Knossos was visibly shifting: each room and even segments of the hallway were instantly being interchanged and slotted about as Minos willed it. The already labyrinthian structure was becoming that much more mind-bending to navigate.
“Nifty trick.” Hydra noted, “No offense but if he’s going to make this difficult using the palace layout, why don’t we just blow it up and take Minos out in the blast?”
“Because the explosion would take us and the rest of Crete out with it. The generator that channels divine Power into the palace would blow us off the map if detonated. Doubly so for you since it runs on Zeus’ power. So, let’s avoid any big blasts that could detonate it.” Mel explained.
“I see. So, we have to go room by room, dealing with the annoying castle gimmick?” Hydra sighed.
Seeing the saddened look on Mel’s face, he paused before putting it another way.
“This castle is a marvel of the Cretan people. Once we get rid of Minos, this cultural heirloom will be back in the right hands.” Mel’s partner reassured her.
“Whatever. I’m not thin skinned like that. I don’t care if you call it a gimmick. It’s just… the confusing layout of this labyrinth was supposed to keep us safe from attackers… but we were betrayed. One of our own helped Minos and his men maneuver through the palace. It’s fitting that we turn the tables.” Mel explained.
“But then… if he wanted to avoid a repeat of last time, what’s the over under that he’s shifting the rooms around to lead us to a trap?” Hydra asked as he and Mel turned a corner.
They came face to face with another platoon of mercenaries firing blasts at them. As the explosion blazed out, the soldiers from behind caught up and began firing as well. The entire hallways filled with dust and debris but as it cleared, Mel and Hydra just walked out, completely unharmed. They had not stopped because of the lasts… they had stopped to check their map!
“That’s strange, that path should have been through the immutable rooms. We should be on our way to the actual safehouse where they all are.” Mel grumbled as she took out the map she had drawn before.
“Maybe I should be the one giving the tour.” The serpent snickered.
“Quiet you. I know my way around: this is my house.” Mel grumbled.
“Correction! This palace as well as all of Crete are under new management! My management: Minos master of the colony of Minoa!” Minos declared over the fancy PA system he installed. His guests in the safe house all clapped in awe at this other expensive piece of divine tech he acquired.
“Minos!” Mel cried.
“Correct. And you are the disgraced ex-princess back to cause trouble. What’s wrong? Here to avenge your daddy?” Minos laughed.
“I’m here to avenge all of Crete and free it from you swine!” Mel cried.
“You and what army? Because I have mine. Talk to me once you’ve dealt with them.” Minos chuckled before hanging up.
He sat on his throne with his noble guests as he used his surveillance system to play the battles events on the walls. It would be dinner and a show: the slaughtering of the pests that dared interrupt his fancy dinner.
Mel and Hydra looked at the troops surrounding them, nonplussed. The troops ran to attack, swinging their weapons and in short watching them all shatter on the two demigods’ powerful bodies and armor. Crete’s Champion and Lerna’s Labor didn’t need to fight back, they just stood there as the mob of mercenaries tired themselves out.
“You guys done yet?” Hydra asked as an exhausted soldier broke his fist on the serpent’s face.
Now they all just stumbled around, panting for breath.
“I’ll take that as a yes. Say, do you know the way to your boss? It’d be much easier to kill him if we knew the way and my friend here doesn’t know the layout as well as she claimed.” Hydra whispered to him loud enough for Mel to hear.
“Oh, shut up!” Mel smacked her partner through the wall in anger.
That gigantic swing was so fast and had such weight and force behind it… that it would turn any in that army to paste. Each one of them backed away, terrified of the foe their boss had trapped them with.
“Geez Mel, you’ve gotta get that temper under control. If it wasn’t me you hit, you could’ve done serious damage. Save that anger for Minos.” Hydra laughed off the hit as he walked out from the rubble, still unharmed. If that attack couldn’t hurt him nothing the mercs could do stood a chance!
With the walls Minos put up in front of and behind them all: Hydra and Mel weren’t trapped in there with the army… the army was trapped in there with them!
