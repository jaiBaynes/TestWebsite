*Myth 15.64: The People’s Court

Mel had already been sentenced to death but then it got worse! Forced to play by the rules of the trial, Mel and Hydra were powerless as the kings carried out their kangaoo court. Crime after crime, both racked up insane, numerous death penalties. The kings listed in meticulous detail all the crimes of the villains against the nobles.
“And what of the nobles’ crimes against the villains?” Hydra asked.
“What?” the judges were confused.
“Y’know I’ve always found it funny… the terms noble and villain are so stupid. To be noble is to be good? What is good about the actual nobles!? The king and his cronies of every polis of every part of this Kosmos effect eviller than all the people combined! And to be a villain, what does that mean? The word villain literally means to be a low-born commoner: an average person from a village! But they have no power to effect evil on any meaningful scale. The worst villain can never harm as many people as the tamest wicked noble. Just by existing as nobles, you rob your people and your slaves of their freedom and the fruits of their labor!” Hydra declared before the court.
The room was silent for a moment.
“As everyone in this court room was just witness to, you just committed seditious conspiracy. For that crime, Hydra son of Typhon, you are sentenced to death!” the Judges convicted him instantly.
“Well, you tried.” Mel just shrugged.
“No! This isn’t over. Screw your rules and screw you! Law only has any merit when it has the consent of the governed. You self serving parasites just make up whatever rule benefits you and force it on everyone else! You never punish your own misdeeds, and your crimes are all but exclusive to those under you! If anything, you should be on trial!” Hydra continued soapboxing.
“You would put us on trial!? Sacred king judges chosen by Zeus himself?” the Judges laughed at him.
Hydra did not back down as his eyes burned with conviction. That face: that proof that he believed every word he said made the kings laugh even harder.
“Do not be a fool! You speak with the naivety of a child! We are the law: we are above the law! What you accuse us of is not a crime, it is not wrong because we say so!” the Judge Kings concluded.
“You idiots are the ones with the intellects of children! Your logic is so stupid, listen to yourselves! If you were on the stand, those judging you would laugh instead!” Hydra cried.
“And who would judge us? None but Zeus himself are in a position to do so.” The Judges scoffed.
“Wrong!!!” Hydra pointed at them accusingly!
The nobles were shocked! What was he on about.
“So, Mel, we waste enough time yapping to recharge your batteries?” Hydra smirked.
“Oh, so you were doing that on purpose?” Mel chuckled.
“It is no use! No violence is permitted in our court. Even at full strength, you cannot break the hold of our Microcosm!” the Tribunal roared.
“You’re right. This is a very strong Microcosm; it’s three in one. Mel and I doing one of our own aren’t enough.” Hydra grinned as he and Mel began glowing with Power!
“That’s right. But you old farts spent a long time yapping. You loved hearing yourselves talk for too long because this idiot has been cooking up the perfect way to kill you.” Mel grinned.
The Judges gasped. Those two… they had been silently communicating using telepathy. Of all the audacity! They’d been secretly plotting instead of listening to the judges’ ruling!
“Whatever you try it won’t work! We have 3 Microcosms too your 2!!!” the Judges screamed at this point mostly to calm themselves down.
“Your Microcosm is indeed strong… I think I’ll take it!” Hydra grinned.
Mel summoned her Beehive and Hydra summoned his Lernia Lake, but a third Microcosm also emerged. Hydra, the absolute mad lad burned up the last of his Power to achieve an unheard-of feat: he created two Microcosms at the same time!!!
Hydra’s Microcosm allowed him to store abilities that had nearly killed him in the past, but if an ability was currently active, he could bypass the specifications and copy it no matter what! Although he would lose access to the copied power as soon as he stopped using it. The villain’s bag of tricks never seemed to end!
Now three on three, the two sides of artificial Universes fought back and forth for control of reality. Space and time itself crashed and rolled like fierce tidal waves! The three judge brothers were horrified as their united Microcosm crumbled before Mel and Hydras’! The kings fell from their high judge sets and were now at the feet of Mel and Hydra!
It was true: they were now on the other end of their own Microcosm: the Divine Tribunal.
“Welcome to the People’s Court!” Hydra corrected their assumption. The kings were shocked.
Hydra had not only copied their ability but altered it. Rather than him and Mel sitting as lone Judges, surrounding the terrified nobles was a truly massive jury of their subjects. No, in the People’s Court all were peers and this jury of peers had some getting even to do.
Instead of being room sized, Hydra exhausted himself by pushing to the limit and creating a giant court room that brings in all the citizens of Crete. The crooked nobles were now the ones acting as defendants with Mel as prosecutor and all the people of Crete as the jury. Witness after witness came to the stand attesting to how the king and his cronies exploited the land and abused the people.
It was not looking good. The kings in their defense said much the same as they had to Hydra previously. The jury was outraged. When logic failed to justify their cruel hierarchy, the nobles fell back on what they always did: the threat of their monopoly on violence.
The nobles shouted at their subjects threatening them with all the horrible punishments they will dole out once this is over. For siding with villains and terrorists the kings would kill them all!
“Now then. People of Crete, having heard all that it is time to vote. Shall the kings continue to have power over you and abuse you and steal what you worked for with your own hands? Or shall we people of Crete say down with this colonizing monarchy? Shall we be rid of these parasites once and for all?” Mel turned to her peers and asked.
The kings were horrified. Right after threatening to kill the people who actually held their lives in their hands, the kings realized how screwed they were. Unsurprisingly, the people Crete voted that the monarchy was to be done away with.
With the will of the people behind her, Mel took her mighty hammer and strolled towards the convicted criminals.
“In the people’s court there are no judges like you. You’ve met your Jury and heard what they say. That just leaves me: your executioner.” Mel ominously said while raising her hammer aloft. A bolt of lightning came down and struck it, charging the weapon up for the kill!
“I am Melissa of Crete. For killing my father, for taking my home… prepare to die.” Melissa declared as she raised her murderous gavel.
“Wait! How much! How much do you want!? We have money!” the kings cried.
“All that you have, you stole from your subjects it isn’t yours to give.” Melissa retorted.
“Power! Beyond your wildest imaginings! All we have and more, we could give you dominion over Crete- restore you as the new queen!” the kings cried.
“I am Melissa of Crete daughter of Melisseus, but I have no interest in your throne. I am a representative, not a ruler. And I must represent the people’s will to see you destroyed!” Melissa refused them again.
“Anything you desire! Any wish in your heart we can call upon Mount Olympus and Zeus and every other god to grant it! We have friends in high places- we could have you become a god yourself!” the kings pleaded again.
Melissa paused. The kings grinned, they thought they had your in.
“Anything. Ask for anything you desire, Queen Melissa! Tell us what you desire! Anything you want!” they groveled at her feet as they were used to groveling before the gods.
“I want my father back.” Melissa said before bringing the hammer down and smiting all three wretched masters.
