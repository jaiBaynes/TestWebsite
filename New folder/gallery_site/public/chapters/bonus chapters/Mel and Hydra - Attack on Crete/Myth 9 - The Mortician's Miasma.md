*Myth 15.57: The Mortician’s Miasma
“Money well spent, brother.” Sarpedon laughed as Minos rushed to take the display off the corpses of his two hired henchmen.
“It seems victory is not as certain as you thought.” Rhadamanthus added.
“I… Asterion! What is the meaning of this! You said they had Epiphanies! Doesn’t that make them invincible!?” Minos demanded.
“Those two were the weakest of my men. They were necessary sacrifices to discover the abilities of our enemies. However, they have proven themselves very nutritious to us indeed. On top of dealing damage to our opponents and thus wasting their Power to regenerate, they have also exposed some of our prey’s hidden abilities.” Asterion spoke heartlessly about his own fallen fighters.
“I see… then the next wave will be able to finish them off?” Minos asked.
“Why not send the rest of the Neo-Kuretes to finish the job?” a squeamish nobleman among Minos’ cronies asked.
“Because, we still do not have a fair gauge of their full capacity. All Neo-Kuretes are on standby to attack, but to be safe only one should engage each attacker at a time. We don’t know if they have abilities that excel at facing multiple opponents such as manipulation or illusions or… a Microcosm.” Asterion explained his strategy.
“A Microcosm? The highest form of Power usage?” Minos cried in shock.
Only the greatest gods and their chosen few had such advanced abilities. If there was a chance these two invaders had such abilities, Asterion was right to be cautious.
“Lord Minos… communications have been disabled! We have been unable to contact Olympus.” A servant whispered to the king.
“Damn… they must have inside people.” Minos growled.
“No matter. The strongest of the Neo-Kuretes yet remain. We will need no outside help.” Asterion confidently grinned.
The separated Mel and Hydra continued their search for Minos by heading towards the highest level of Power they could sense. Worst case scenario they found each other, better case they found Minos and his cronies: the targets of this assassination gone mostly off the rails.
Mel kept running until she reached a dead end. What a pain. She turned around but found the door closed behind her. Then she realized: she’d just run into the holding cell made for Titans! The original goal of the Palace was to lead Titans into this very impenetrable cell. Mel smashed the Adamant walls with her hammer but barely put a dent in it. The room was meant for Typhon himself, of course it would be an ordeal to break out of.
However, before she could attack again, a loud voice came over the loudspeaker of the cell.
“Murderer! You are a murderer!” the voice cried.
“Yeah, and if you’re with those frauds abusing the Kuretes name, I’ll be killing you next!” Mel cried back.
“Yes, a murderer who plans to kill again… truly you are overflowing with miasma…” the voice cackled.
In Graiac thought, miasma was a kind of evil religious taint that emanated from murderers and attempted murderers. It could infect and contaminate anyone who was even in the same room as them. Analogous to concepts such as “sin”, this stain of guilt was even thought to bring upon bad luck disease and death to those it attached to. Though it was mere superstition, sure to be disproved as Graia advanced during the bronze age, it was seen as fact.
Of course, Miasma wasn’t real… but Power made it so! Mel the murderer began to notice a black fog filling the room. She felt weaker and weaker as the poison was slowly killing her in the confined room. In a panic she began hammering the wall harder and harder… but to less and less effect as she sank to the ground in pain and agony.
“Murderer, your own Miasma shall be the end of you!” the voice over the speaker cackled at the sight of Mel dying.
Mel was furious! But the unbearable pain made her sluggish and she could barely think straight. This was the most pain she’d ever experienced!
“What strong Miasma you have! How many people have killed to accumulate this much?” the voice continued to mock her, but now the sound was distorted by the thick miasma in the air.
“I’ll kill as many as I have to in order to set this right.” Mel struggled back to her feet.
Even moving she felt great resistance now from all the Miasma flooding the room. Then Mel got an idea. She felt the pressure closing in on her but that also meant…
“Surrender and die!” the man blared.
However, Melissa instead raised her hammer and instead of hitting the wall again, began to spin around with it! Using her centrifugal force, she spun faster and faster until the created a tornado!
“What do you think you’re doing? Whatever it is, it’s a waste of time!” the voice began.
Then, the point where she hit the wall began to crack. The air pressure in the room was not just air any more. All that massive Miasma was pressing down on her… but it also pressed against the walls. Mel’s tornado was upping that pressure significantly and that weak point she’d made in the wall was buckling under said pressure.
“Stop! What are you doing!?” the man panicked.
But it was too late, the internal pressure shattered the cell and the Miasma flooded out, engulfing the man whose ability spawned it to begin with, he instantly died to his own poison, and it died with him. Mel was left panting and coughing as her body slowly regenerated. That wouldn’t be enough to keep her from her target.
