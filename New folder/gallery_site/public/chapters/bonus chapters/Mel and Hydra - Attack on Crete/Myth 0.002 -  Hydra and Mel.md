*Myth 15.51: Hydra and Mel

Hydra and Melissa had arrived on the scene. Dressed to impress, they utterly stunned the insider who had ushered them in.
Melissa, the beautiful princess wore not a dress or gown but rather armor. She had returned here with a vengeance. The avenging bee queen now looked the part: clad in black and yellow Adamant armor.
The nighttime assailants’ outfit was predominantly black as pitch. Her black steel breastplate went above her black leather skier which itself concealed the top of her black wool leggings. Breaking up the monotony, her outfit was fastened with solid gold bands: perfect conduits of the divine sparks coursing around her. The wool leggings and leather boots, unbecoming and alien to ancient Graiac fashion kept her insulated while the gold ornaments and highlights allowed her the perfect release points for the storm within. If her very outfit did not keep her contain, she threatened to explode with divine thunder. No, she needed to stay cool and composed… until it was finally time to strike.
Over her shoulder, she hoisted a massive black and gold hammer of comical size and mass. Her weapon was practically the size of her whole body but with her Herculean strength, held it aloft with one arm. Woe to any she hit with that.
The hammer’s symbol and indeed the golden insignia on her breastplate was the bumblebee. Fitting her name, the bee woman also had a cape cut in the shape of apiary wings. However, her sting was not a mere point: it was that of high voltage lightning.
Yet what truly concerned the Cretan aiding and abetting the two was the man who came with the prince. That serpent man with the princess… he looked utterly sinister. He was coated heat to toe in distinctive black and red.
Though heavily armored, the suit made several functional compromises in exchange for aesthetics. His breastplate was formfitting and indented to show off his stunning muscles. All around him, the armor was serrated and covered in a grid pattern to mimic more natural armor such as a serpent’s scales. All these intentional weak points in the suit were visually appealing but weak points, nonetheless. What kind of fool would dress up caring more about looking cool than actually protecting himself?
Hydra was as handsome as Mel was beautiful, though his seductive smirk was both alluring and intimidating. His demon red eyes almost felt hypnotic, as though his beauty could command any victim to dance to the snake’s whims. His flowing black hair and beard were the peak of Graiac masculinity, yet they were unmistakably a match for the villain Typhon. Yet to some that dangerous look only added to his appeal.
From directly out of his forehead seemed to grow sharp red horns. Too small to be weapons, but still sharp enough to be imposing. There was no fastening wire or rope that bound them; they were affixed to nothing. How then did the features of a monster sit atop those of a man? Did they grow out of him, like a brief glimpse of the dragon peering out?
Hydra’s beautiful face sat atop a body clad in dragon armor. Though he had the limbs of man, around his limbs, waist and even neck coiled demonic metal snakes, so realistic and tangible that they threatened to come alive! The otherworldly material he wore seemed almost like living shadows. The steel armor wrapped around him… wasn’t regular material or even normal Adamant for that matter. The dragon’s chthonic scales twisted and contorted into a pleasing shape. Truly there was a beastly character masquerading as a man.
His flowing red cape blew in the wind, so long and billowing he could conceal himself in it as a create cloak. The cape too moved like cloth… but on closer inspection was also the dragon’s scales playing pretend. Black and red from head to toe, Hydra’s mysterious attire was simultaneous attention grabbing and mystifyingly distracting from what any poor soul who crossed him should really have worried about.
Conversely to mini-Mel’s massive mallet, huge Hydra held a hilariously half-pint sickle. Each was unnatural and odd but together they seemed to balance the other out. As the portal closed behind them, Mel and Hydra strode forth confidently in the heart of enemy territory.
“Well done, Thales. The swine are all assembled on the top floor as expected?” Mel asked.
“Yes princess.” The servant reported.
“Nice. Window access and everything. This is going to be easy.” Hydra grinned.
“Let’s just get in, kill them and get out.” Mel said while lifting her massive hammer: Mylopetra.
“But princess Melissa, it is worse than we feared. Just before you arrived, the cruel king ordered the protestors to all be killed!” the servant pleaded with the princess.
Melissa paused.
“Shouldn’t killing the king put a stop to that?” Hydra asked.
“No… if we fight the king discretely, his troops will have no reason to stop. As we killed Minos in secret, all those people would die too.” Mel noted.
“But if we go and save them, our element of surprise will be given away.” Hydra noted.
“And the mission becomes much harder… and uncertain.” Mel thought out loud.
“Well, what would you have us do? This is your home after all. You take point.” Mel’s partner in crime deferred to her. The she-bee was given pause.
At the front gate of the palace, the doors opened as the troops exited. A sobbing woman grabbed at the feet of the troops, begging for any food they could offer her starving baby. Before long countless other villagers offered similar plights.
But the troops of Minos were not Cretan natives. They were contract killers from Graia: mercenaries who had no attachment to any of these people. That was what made them the perfect jackbooted thugs of the colonizing crown. Befitting his role, one soldier prepared to step on the head of the first begging woman.
She closed her eyes, begging for her life as she braced for death. Instead, it was the soldier who cried out in pain as he was crushed! Dropping from the sky in a bolt of lightning, Mel pushed him down with her massive hammer, holding it just enough that the weight left him alive. The crushed man cried for his broke body and now he was the one begging for relief.
“Shame on all of you. Coming out here to kill all these innocent people. I have half a mind to crush you all instead.” Mel said while letting a little more of the hammer down to crush the soldier further.
The other troops grew terrified. With one hand she held a hammer whose leftover weight was enough to nearly flatten one of them. But there was just one of her and an army of them. The troops raised their blades and charged them with Power. Up close she was a beast but from a distance, they could blast her and the rioters to kingdom come!
The soldiers all fired massive infernos of power at the screaming commoners. But when the dust settled, Mel didn’t have a scratch on her, and a great black wall shielded all the innocents from the explosion.
“This your second chance to flee with your lives. We aren’t here to kill you. Don’t make us. We are the good guys after all.” Hydra warned as he appeared as if from nowhere. He said that with the most intimidating, evil grin imaginable.
“Get out of our way. Only Minos and his cronies have to die.” Melissa declared as she lifted her hammer from the half-crushed mercenary and pointed it at his fellows.
