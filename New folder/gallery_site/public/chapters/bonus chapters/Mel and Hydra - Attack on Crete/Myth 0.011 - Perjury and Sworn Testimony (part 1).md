*Myth 15.59: Perjury and Sworn Testimony 1/2

“They lost! What failures!” Minos whined.
“No. They did exactly what was planned.” Asterion explained. Everyone looked at him in utter bafflement.
“Those two were perfect: they weakened their targets while confirming key weaknesses of theirs. Now the weakened prey stumbles into a trap and we go for the kill.” Asterion smiled.
As he said that both Mel and Hydra were shocked to be engulfed by a bright light. The two had almost no time to recover after their previous battles. As soon as they entered the next room, they were each pulled into an enemy Microcosm!
Microcosms: artificial realms constructed by only the most elite of Power users. In their own little worlds, their rules and powers were absolute. Any fighter faced by this predicament had a tough battle ahead of them.
This was the worst possible scenario for most fighters; however, Hydra actually breathed a sigh of relief. He was in enemy territory but at least that damned Solaris Power was finally off him. Hydra could breathe easily as his body repaired itself from the damage that direct exposure did.
“Thanks man. I know your probably brought me in here to kill me, but this is actually a huge help.” Hydra smiled with his energy restored.
“Don’t thank me yet villain.” The stuffy Prosecutor before him refused the warm welcome. His Microcosm was a great court room with Hydra in place of a defendant and himself on the prosecutor’s spot.
Mel found herself in a similar situation, a near identical courtroom Microcosm however while she was also in the defendant’s spot, the one who dragged her into it was instead in the place of a witness. The sleazy looking witness grinned at her, much more welcomingly than Hydra’s foe.
“Welcome to your trial miss killer. I’m glad you made it. It’s just a shame that I see you forgot that hammer of yours outside.” The witness shrugged.
Mel was confused. What was he talking about? Then she looked to her hand and her hammer was gone! But how!? She was sure she was holding it just a second ago. Was he somehow able to force her to leave it behind when he pulled her into the Microcosm? No! She was sure, just a second ago, even in the Microcosm she was holding it. While Mel remained baffled, the witness testified again.
“And I see all those nasty wounds you took in the previous battle. They sure did a number on you, didn’t they?” the witness shook his head.
Mel was confused. She was pretty hurt in that last fight, but she was able to regenerate from most of that damage. Her healing factor was nowhere near as advanced as Hydra but still she was mostly fine by the time she was pulled into the Microcosm.
Then Mel fell over, covered in horrible wounds! Most of those weren’t even wounds she received from previous battles! What was going on!?
Meanwhile, Hydra punched the wall of the court room Microcosm to no avail. Those weren’t just walls; they were the ends of this literal pocket-Universe. If he wanted out, he needed to beat the Microcosm’s master.
“I’m getting sick of these gimmick abilities. Alright, what’s your schtick?” Hydra groaned.
“By entering the court of law, you acknowledge you will tell the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth.” The Prosecutor reminded him.
“I could clash Microcosms with him to get out, but that would waste a ton of Power and I’m not even to Minos yet…” Hydra ignored him.
“Answer the prosecution! This is the last warning you shall receive!” the Prosecutor declared.
“What?” Hydra asked.
“Answer this intruder, why are you here in Crete?” the prosecutor asked.
“Well, I’ve been looking for a delightful little summer home like all the other nobles with villas here.” Hydra joked only to immediately be struck by lightning. 
Hydra fell to his knees, utterly stunned by the cacophony of pain he was in. It was even worse than being next to that machine blasting out Zeus Power. No… that lightning must have been from Zeus himself.
“All prosecutors are servants of Zeus and his Cult of Justice. If you do not respond truthfully to the prosecution, I can smite you by the Power vested in me by the god of justice!” the Prosecutor pointed accusingly.
“Great…” Hydra groaned as he struggled to stand after such a blast took him by surprise.
“I ask again. Why are you here in Crete?” the prosecutor demanded.
“To kill the imperialist swine who have taken Mel’s home from her.” A very angry Hydra replied. He was so terrifying that the watching nobles felt a shiver down their spine.
He leapt at his foe, but the prosecutor demanded, “What is your next attack?”
Hydra ignored him but was struck again! He fell to his knees. Again, he was taken by surprise and his entire body was in utter agony.
“I told you before. You had warning. Refusing to answer the prosecution shall be punished with a divine smite. Zeus himself granted me this Power and I don’t think you can survive much more of it.” The Prosecutor warned.
Hydra leapt forward again, more sluggish than before due to his injury.
“What is your next attack?” the prosecutor demanded once more.
“I’m going to gore you!” Hydra swung his sickle at the enemy’s stomach making him block there. At the last minute, Hydra switched targets to try and decapitate him. Another bolt struck him.
“Need I remind you, you are to say the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth?” the prosecutor demanded while looking at the half-dead Hydra.
Mel was in similar dire straits. She ran towards her opponent, but the perjurer just laughed.
“I can’t believe I saw you just trip and break your nose like that.” He laughed.
Mel tripped and fell, breaking her nose as he said. The perjurer cackled, clearly enjoying himself. It was clear he was just playing with her. If he wanted to kill her, he probably could. Mel reached the same conclusion as Hydra: this was a bad matchup. They had no other choice…
