*Myth 15.595: Perjury and Sworn Testimony 2/2

As Asterion watched with the rest of Minos’ party he smiled, “Now they shall have no choice but to exhaust themselves and reveal their own Microcosms to escape.”
Mel and Hydra knew just what to do. They began to summon their own Microcosm to clash with their opponents’! However, to Asterion’s shock, they didn’t fully manifest them! Instead, they did just enough to dispel the enemy Microcosm around themselves. They just needed a few cubic meters of their own territory.
Then, since they weren’t being blocked by the Palace of Knossos’ anti-teleportation field nor the Microcosms, they could do it. Completely independently of each other, both partners in crime activated their team ability: Partners in Crime. Just as they could teleport to their accomplice on Crete at the start of all this, they could teleport to each other.
Hydra moved to swap places with Mel as she did the same to him! Now their opponents were shocked: the Perjurer was face to face with Hydra and the Prosecutor was face to face with Mel!
“So… what’s your annoying gimmick?” Hydra asked as he faced down the perjurer.
“I liked the little girl better. But I see that now you’ve lost your legs and aren’t much taller than her.” He grinned. What he said immediately happened to Hydra.
“How did you get here?” the prosecutor demanded. Mel ignored him and was struck by lightning. 
Both servants of Minos thought they’d won but were horrified: Hydra regrew his legs instantly and Mel was completely unharmed by the lightning: if anything, it had powered her up!
The perjurer panicked and began to describe horrible things happening to Hydra, but he just walked through it unphased. His body was being ripped apart, but Hydra didn’t even react. The increasingly exasperated perjurer described gorier and gorier details, but Hydra still approached him and punched him in the face, breaking his jaw so he couldn’t speak anymore. As the man tried and failed to beg for mercy, Hydra roasted him alive with his flaming eyes.
“Thanks for the recharge!” Mel roared as she overflowed with incredible divine Power! The sparky blaze shook the Prosecutor to his core.
Mel then held her hand out, focussing Power into it. The Prosecutor panicked. Was she going to strike him with lightning now that she’d built up charge? Or what was her special ability… there were too many variables that had entered the equation with this new fighter.
“What is your next attack!” he panicked, hoping to figure her out.
“I will kill you.” Mel replied with a look of death in her eyes.
No lightning came down, after all she had not perjured herself. Now the Prosecutor’s panic went into overdrive. He had to prove her wrong! He had to survive and make her perjure herself. But what was her attack? A blast or a punch or-
The Prosecutor, oh so focussed on Mel, failed to react when her massive hammer came blasting through the wall of his courtroom. It was attracted to matter of Mel’s hand like electrons seek out an opposing charge! Woe to anything that got in its way. The Prosecutor was utterly destroyed as the massive hammer blasted through him and flew into Mel’s hand. She wasn’t lying: she had killed him with that attack.
Both Microcosms fell and Mel and Hydra emerged victorious to everyone’s shock. Minos looked to his mentor Asterion in shock.
“Unexpected but nothing of value was lost. With that, we now have everything we need to secure victory, don’t we?” Asterion chuckled to himself as a mysterious soldier entered the room.
Mel and Hydra continued searching for their target, but little did they know, everything they faced so far was just the opening warmups. Now their true enemies had all the information they need to engage the weakened duo.
“This is ridiculous! We should evacuate!” a noble finally had it up to here with the failures of their protectors.
“Fools of little faith!” Minos cried.
“Those two are clearly unstoppable, we should flee while we still can! Everyone else failed, why should we think those last two you sent will fare any better?” another nervous noble added.
“Do you not know who the leader of the Neo-Kuretes is?” Sarpedon demanded.
The other nobles were shocked. Normally he’d side with anyone who went against his brother. Yet even he backed him up this time.
“Asterion is not just our mentor. He is a god: a warrior god who defeated countless Titans during the war. He is not to be underestimated.” Rhadamanthus agreed with his brothers for once.
“Even getting him off Olympus is a rarity. I practically had to beg him to get him to attend today’s party. All those other mooks you’ve seen are merely his minions.” Minos added.
“Very well. He is strong, but can he really defeat both of them alone?” a noblewoman asked.
“He won’t have to. The great Asterion has a score to settle with the son of his old enemy Typhon. As for the daughter of Melisseus the perfect opponent to defeat her is on the way to intercept.” Minos chuckled to himself.
Hydra meanwhile opened the door at the end of the hallway, only to immediately by blasted by Solaris radiation! He winced, stumbling back as it ran its course on his body.
“Incredible. In person, you really do look just like Typhon. You share his weakness after all.” Asterion said as he walked out from the generator room with a confident grin.
“Yeah, everyone always says that. That’s what stuns me the most about hearing how he’d tough it out and fight them anyways. You have no idea how painful this is.” Hydra laughed.
“It’s about to get much worse.” Asterion remarked before punching Hydra in the face.
At the same time, Melissa stopped in her tracks: utterly shocked and horrified to find Prodotes, her father’s former right-hand man still wearing his Kuretes armor standing before her.
She remembered him from her childhood. He was her father’s right-hand man, he was a dearly close family friend, he was a…
“Traitor!!!” Mel screamed as her Power spiked.
