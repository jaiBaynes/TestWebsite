*Myth 15.58: None Shall Pass

Meanwhile Hydra was stopped not by a wall, but by a man standing in his path.
“Oh great, do you know how I get to Minos? I’m kind of in a race with my pal to find him and want bragging rights.” Hydra grinned.
“None shall pass!” the Judge barring his way declared.
“What?” Hydra was confused by that non-answer.
“None shall pass!” The Judge repeated.
“…I’m trying to be nice and not killing you. Now step aside or I’ll move you.” Hydra reached out but was shocked. His hand kept moving forward but never reached the Judge.
“I move for no man. I am a Judge, and a Judge is Sacrosanct: not to be touched. How dare you even think of touching me.” The Judge scoffed.
Hydra lowered his hand and fired his flaming eyes. They too seemed to hit a wall and couldn’t reach the judge. A frustrated Hydra began throwing a series of punches and kicks, none of which could pass the impenetrable barrier.
“Fool! I am untouchable!” the Judge laughed.
“So, it would seem.” Hydra laughed back.
Hydra couldn’t get past him so naturally he just waved, “Bye.”
He turned around to leave and try travelling down a different path, but the shifting palace immediately positioned the Judge and his impenetrable wall before him again. Any direction Hydra turned; the room morphed to block his way with them. Normally he could out speed the shifting palace but when only controlling a single room it was able to beat him to it.
“Well, this is annoying.” Hydra groaned.
Then he felt a surge of pain burning his back. He turned around, and behind him was the generator room with the giant reactor full of Solaris Power! Wherever he fled, Minos made sure it followed.
“Very annoying!” Hydra groaned.
“Sorry, you cannot go back or forward. I shall simply block your path until the divine light of Zeus finishes eradicating your demonic filth.” The Judge declared.
Hydra began to sweat, partially from all the radiation and partially because he was actually in a bind. Then they played the waiting game. Hydra couldn’t escape and he couldn’t kill his enemy either. He just sat there scheming in a race against the clock as his body began visibly dying from exposure.
“Yikes.” The Judge said after looking up from his finished scroll. He was so absorbed in his reading, he’d failed to notice how much he was winning by doing nothing. The immediate contrast made the Judge flinch at the sight.
“I’m sorry the sight of me decaying is so uncomfortable for you.” Hydra rolled his eyes.
“That Solaris stuff… it isn’t killing me too, is it?” the Judge asked.
Hydra paused, realizing something. As he got an idea he got a devious little grin, annoying his foe.
“What?” the Judge demanded.
“Nothing. It’s just that shield of yours is impenetrable, isn’t it? What would you have to worry about?” Hydra grinned before throwing his sickle at the Judge. The sickle bounced off and back into Hydra’s hands.
“Nothing. Nothing can get past my barrier.” The Judge declared before unfurling his next scroll to read while waiting for Hydra to die. Meanwhile, Hydra was throwing his sickle at the wall over and over to little success. The annoyed Judge just scoffed.
“It’s no use. You can’t get out and I just have to sit here reading Jurisprudence Monthly until you fry. So, stop annoying me and go peacefully!” The Judge grinned.
“Law stuff? Really? I’m more a political science and science-science guy myself.” Hydra explained.
“Well, you shall take that knowledge with you to the grave.” The Judge said as he continued reading his scroll.
“Aw, but sharing is caring. In fact, I’ve got a fun fact for you. My biological father Typhon was something of a scientist. After bonding with the Goddess of the World Terra, he gained profound insight into how the World worked. It turns out that everything in the Universe is actually made up of these tiny things he called ‘particles.’ Except when they’re acting like waves.” Hydra explained.
“Very nice.” The Judge ignored him.
“When you smell something that’s because particles from it have entered your nose.” Hydra explained.
“And what’s your point? I shall smell your rotting corpse soon enough.” The Judge noted.
“You’ll smell my particles sooner than that. I’ve been farting up a storm.” Hydra said apropos of nothing.
“Foul creature! You are before a judge in a sacred palace, and you make such crude excretions!” the judge panicked as he realized the smell and covered his nose. Now that Hydra mentioned it, that smelled awful!
“Disgusting! A healthy man shouldn’t smell like that! Get thee to a doctor at once!!!” the Judge bellowed with a pinched nose.
“Sorry about that, it’s just that I noticed something. See, light is also a particle, except when it isn’t. You can only see me because light particles are bouncing off me and into your eyes.” Hydra noted.
“So?” the Judge demanded.
“And you can only hear me because my vibrations through the air’s particles enter your ears.” Hydra grinned.
“Get to the point! You’ll die before you get to it at this point!” the judge growled.
“You’re the one with the short life expectancy.” Hydra grinned.
The judge panicked as he held his nose to keep the smell out… only to notice his nose bleeding. He was coughing and burning up! What was happening! The Judge collapsed, vomiting and bleeding profusely!
“Your barrier won’t let any attacks through. Such, a complex ability takes all your focus and Power. That means that anything that gets through the barrier can attack you without you being able to muster any defence.” Hydra smiled.
“But how!? What can pass my barrier!?” the dying Judge demanded.
“Weren’t you listening to anything I said? Particles. With each flatulent release I warned you about, I attached some of my Adamant constructs. These bad boys would enter your body when you breathed in and do their work on your insides. After all, with all your Power spent on this indestructible barrier, you need to breathe air to power your body. Little did you know, what you breathed in didn’t just smell bad, it was quite detrimental to your health. Just for you, I call this new technique my Silent But Deadly attack.” Hydra laughed to himself.
The Judge could hardly believe it. This was how he was going to die? Being farted to death!? Unfortunately, those were the brakes, he died and his barrier with him, allowing Hydra to progress.
Both Mel and Hydra were off again, a little worse for wear but ready to face whatever they faced next!
