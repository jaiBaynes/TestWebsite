*Myth 15.61: Hydra vs Asterion

“You’re a real headache, you know that!?”, the weakened Hydra growled in pain as Asterion was crushing his head between his hands!
“I fought in the Titan war you know!” Asterion boasted.
“Very impressive…” Hydra groaned, “with that little Zeus battery in the other room doing most of the work for you, you can in fact beat up a half dead man.” Hydra chuckled as he slowly pried his enemy’s hands from his head.
“Shut up, boy. Strategy is part of battle.” Asterion insisted before kicking Hydra in the stomach.
“Was sending your boys after me as cannon fodder part of strategy too?” Hydra stumbled backwards, while trying and failing to fire his flaming eyes.
“It was. It made me a hero. I’m no pansy that hides behind a gimmick power. Could’ve been an Olympian if I wanted to.” Asterion grinned proudly at his impotent opponent as he punched him in the face and swept his legs.
Hydra woozily fell over but retained enough strength the flip back to his feet in no time.
“Really? Because that’s not what I heard. I heard that very antic of treating your men as disposable got you in some deep shit. Heard Neptune knocked you down a peg and nearly kicked you out of the army altogether.” Hydra grinned with bloodied teeth.
Asterion took exception to that and grabbed Hydra by the throat before throwing him even closer to the source of Solaris Power. No sooner had Hydra stood up than Asterion punched him through a bronze statue of a great bull which represented Zeus. Two such bulls stood guard next to the generator which drew its Power from Zeus. After smashing the one, Asterion wasted no time before slamming Hydra through the other with great strength.
“I could break most gods in two with my bare hands! Don’t mess with me!” the prideful soldier kicked Hydra closer to his weakness so he could torture him further.
As the dying Hydra tried to crawl away, Asterion dragged him by his foot closer still to the source of his weakness. The god threw the serpent directly into the sparking divine light. Hydra screamed in pain as he fell limply to the floor. He crawled away from it but couldn’t get very far as Asterion just laughed.
“That must hurt.” Asterion kicked Hydra back towards the giant battery. The sea serpent screamed again as he made direct contact with it.
“Why not bring out that little Microcosm of yours to get away from it?” Asterion asked.
“Nah… I don’t need it.” Hydra grinned.
Focussing with all his might, Hydra generated a massive wall of Adamant scales around the generator to keep its Solaris light from reaching him.
“Much better.” Hydra stood up as his body recovered.
He’d foregone shielding himself so he could store up enough Power to generate that wall. The tricked Asterion threw a punch at him, but Hydra effortlessly caught his fist! The old god gasped before Hydra returned the favor with a punch square to the face. Asterion stumbled back and punched Hydra in the stomach. Hydra flinched a bit but remained standing and punched Asterion again, toppling him over.
Minos’ mentor coughed up blood and kicked the approaching Hydra but couldn’t even budge him anymore. It was very clear that he had recovered from that cheap trick. Hydra picked Asterion up by his throat before punching him so hard that he was sent flying through the door! Yes, Hydra was still weak and hurting but he felt enough strength return to let him hang with this loser.
With a moment to breathe, Hydra wiped sweat from his brow. Even with that container holding it, Hydra felt burns from the generator and hurried away from it after Asterion. When he ran through the door, he found he was not in the room he entered the generator room from. This was an open courtyard. Asterion floated above with his arms folded.
“How irritating. You are quite strong. It seems you have not been drained enough yet.” Asterion explained.
“Yeah, I’ve been killing your minions all day and I’ve got room for you too, old man!” Hydra declared.
“Such lofty goals. But I am not as weak as those sacrifices you worked your way through earlier.” Asterion laughed.
“That’s how you talk about your own men? You’re disgusting.” Hydra noted.
“Do you expect me to mourn them? Like good soldiers they died for my cause. I would be abandoning my role as their leader if I let pathetic emotions get in the way of using them as effectively as I did. Besides, I shall be avenging them shortly.” Asterion grinned.
“Not likely.” Hydra grinned before firing off his flaming eyes.
Asterion just smiled as out of nowhere, a man leapt up and took the blast for him! Hydra was horrified as an innocent man fell to the ground, burning to death. Hydra ran over to check on him.
“Hey! Are you okay?” Hydra fanned the flames and saw it was a Cretan peasant he’d shot, not a soldier but a civilian.
“What is the meaning of this!? Even now you can’t fight your own battles!?” Hydra demanded.
“An effective leader has people to fight for him. I will make my move when it is time for the killing blow.” He grinned.
“How dare you!” Hydra stood up to fight, only for the room to flood with an army of other people from all walks of life, enslaved by Asterion.
They were all innocent and normally Powerless, yet Asterion’s Power compelled them to fight Hydra. Empowering them, they all fought like demigods and as Hydra tried to talk with them, they beat and battered him.
Hydra was punched so hard he was sent flying into the wall. He stood up as the mob closed around him. He needed to fight them, but he didn’t want to hurt people who couldn’t control their actions. He could see, every action they took was Asterion’s Power puppeteering them like marionettes!
He saw that pompous slave master floating above his minions cackling at the display. The enraged Hydra flew up at him. As a minion jumped up to intercept, Hydra created a body double to tackle him to the ground. Asterion raised an eyebrow, surprised Hydra had yet more tricks to show. No matter, a dozen mor Minions attacked. Hydra created more body doubles out of Adamant to fend them off.
“If you do not stop Hydra, you shall die!” Asterion shout at the next wave of minions that jumped at Hydra.
Hydra dodged them in the same way but was horrified when their bodies exploded in a gory display. He froze, covered in their blood as Asterion smiled.
“You have no time to get sentimental for your enemies. It makes you weak.” Asterion laughed.
“You know… I was only here for Mel at first… but I really REALLY hate you.” Hydra explained.
“That hatred does you no good if you can’t even reach me.” Asterion scoffed.
Hydra flew at Asterion again.
“If you cannot force Hydra to use his Microcosm, you will die!” Asterion shouted at the group of Minions he compelled to attack Hydra next.
As they all closed in on him, the furious Hydra happily obliged. At the same time, with their backs both against the wall both Mel and Hydra activate their Microcosms.
