*Myth 15.56: In a Bind

Mel looked around in confusion. The shifting palace had transported her somewhere else! Curses! She’d allowed herself to get distracted and while was off guard, the palace had moved around her and placed her in some unknown location.
“Damn it! Whatever, Hydra is a big boy, he can take care of himself. And while he distracts them, I’ll take care of Minos.” Mel steeled herself and prepared to move on. But before her stood a similarly robed mysterious man.
“I cannot permit that intruder. King Minos is a great Judge and there shall be no violence in his court.” He declared before making Adamant handcuffs appear on Mel’s arms and force them behind her back!
Minos and his party guests were amazed! Entire armies couldn’t stop Hydra and Mel, but this mysterious fighter had immediately gained the upper hand.
“Behold! Abilities no mere brute force can overcome: the results of Epiphanies!” the leader of the Neo-Kuretes hyped his minions up.
“Epiphanies.” A shocked noblewoman cried.
“Indeed, Epiphanies are special abilities offered as enlightenment only to those chosen by a higher power. No amount of training or raw strength can reproduce these unmatched and divine talents. These types of special powers are exactly what helped us turn the tide of the war against the mighty Titans. Force stands no chance in the face of technique.” Asterion explained for the noncombatants among them.
Minos nodded approvingly. The Neo-Kuretes were worth the price tag: hiring even a single fighter with an Epiphany ability was rare: an entire team of them was game changing. Plus, their leader even helped hype them up to impress Minos’ friends. Was this a thinly veiled advertisement to try and get other buyers among the assembled nobles? Probably, but Minos wasn’t complaining since his ego was boosted too.
Back in the fight, Mel struggled as the restraints on her hands grew tighter and tighter. Even with her Herculian strength, Mel couldn’t break out.
“It’s no use. This ability comes from a higher Power. Learn it well brute: this is an Epiphany that brute strength cannot overcome.” The Bailiff grinned.
Mel stared daggers at him, but no matter how hard she tried, she could fire off electricity. The cuffs had cut off her abilities as well. She could only use her body. Mel clumsily lifted her hammer behind her back and tried in vain to swing it at her foe.
The Bailiff danced around her, dodging each hefty yet clumsy swing with ease. Mel dropped the hammer, finally accepting it was impossible to use this way.
“Is it finally setting in? Is it finally dawning on you that you stand no chance against the Neo-Kuretes!” the Executioner declared.
“The what!?” Mel snapped after hearing the name.
“I am the Executioner of the Neo-Kuretes!” he repeated.
“Neo-Kuretes!? Those bastards from Graia took my, home and my family- then they even took the name sacred to my people!? The pride and joy of native Cretans- the Kuretes is now being appropriated by some Graiacs for merchandizing!?” Mel screamed at him.
Mel was so furious, she felt like she could explode! Thankfully the cuffs restricted her Power and kept her from blowing her top literally. Mel exhaled slowly to calm herself.
“What are you going to do little girl? Your hands are literally tied.” The Bailiff grinned.
“Yeah, these handcuffs are impenetrable, aren’t they.” Mel sighed.
In a flash, she kicked him so hard that his head smashed through the ceiling!
“But your special little power doesn’t do anything about feet, does it?” Mel said before jumping up and kicking him away.
The Bailiff struggled back to his feet. Even a few shots from the superpowered Mel left him in critical condition. What was more, he was horrified to see her casually stretching as she familiarized herself with this new improvised kicking style. His handcuffs were impenetrable… so she was going to kick him to death!? The half-dead Bailiff considered his situation. Her kicks were formidable, but at least she couldn’t use that dangerous hammer of hers anymore…
As soon as he thought that, the Bailiff was mortified to see Mel skillfully use her feet to stand her hammer up and then launch it spinning into the air. As it came back down, the mallet wielder kicked her hammer and launched it like a missile! Like a bolt of lightning, it struck before the Bailiff could even react and utterly obliterated him! The hammer continued blasting down walls as it tore a path through the Palace.
Mel walked past what was left of her pulpy opponent through the path she carved herself. Rather than continue down Minos’ trapped route, she would carve her own right to him…
