*Myth 15.54: The Labyrinth

After literally falling into Minos’ trap, Mel and Hydra found themselves in a deep underground Labyrinth.
“So, Mel, where are we now?” Hydra asked.
“Don’t know. This basement area was built after my father. It seems to be some sick perverted homage to the palace’s maze-like nature.” Mel began.
“Okay. We didn’t kill them, but do we have to help those guys out of this maze too?” the villain asked.
“Let’s just go kill Minos. I’m sure they’re fine.” Mel rolled her eyes.
As soon as she said that, one of the terrified soldiers came running towards them, screaming in utter terror. Mel and Hydra were confused before they saw a gigantic bull monster chasing after him.
Mel grabbed the soldier and leaped back as Hydra took the bull by the horns to stop its rampage. This mighty Monster was strong that even Lernan beast struggled to hold it back.
“Behold the one that will kill you: the great Minotaur! Half man and half beast!!!” Minos Junior cried over the PA.
“Oh yeah! Didn’t your mom sleep with a cow to give birth to this thing? Doesn’t that make this your brother or something?” Hydra burst out laughing as he remembered.
“Shut up and die!” the flustered Minos shrieked before hanging up again.
Hydra laughed so hard that he lost his focus and was bucked by the great Monster. He was thrown back, next to Mel.
“I thought Theseus was supposed to have killed this thing?” Hydra noted as he blew on his aching hands.
“He did. But if it was an Immortal, that means it would come back as an even stronger Monster. If I had to guess, that’s what we’re looking at.” Mel assessed.
Mel was correct. The Minotaur slayed by Theseus was an Immortal by way of his father the Cretan Bull who was by way of his father Typhon! Little did Hydra know that Minos Junior’s brother was his nephew!
The bull rushed and his uncle again caught it head on. But he felt his grip loosening in utter confusion. He should be stronger than that. Then he felt it: his feet were being shocked by something. He felt his strength being drained by the floor but that didn’t make any sense. Unable to overpower the beast, Hydra used its own momentum to toss it over himself and into the walls of the labyrinth.
“Hey Mel… you feel funny at all?” Hydra asked as he stood back up.
“Not really. Why?” Mel asked.
“This place… feels like it is draining me. Is it just me?” the son of Typhon asked.
“Of course… one of the cores channeling Zeus’ Power through this place must be directly below us. I worried this might happen to you given your… stuff with the Solaris. But to think it would have this pronounced of an effect.” Mel had a sudden realization.
“Yeah, on a good day, this Monster would be history already. Even on the upper floor it was fine.” Hydra groaned as he felt pins and needles all over.
Of course, the cell containing the Minotaur, a descendant of Typhon required divine Solaris Power to weaken it. However, given Hydra’s direct descent from Typhon, the weakening effect was much greater.
“What a pain. But oh well, fighting with a handicap makes it a bit more fun.” Hydra grinned while wiping the sweat from his brow. He put on a brave face, but being in such intense Solaris radiation for so long was slowly killing him.
Mel just sighed. She hated that bravado he always wore. Her ally jumped to clash with the Monster again, but this time was completely trampled. Mel watched him laugh as he struggled to get back up. She noticed that he wasn’t healing from the bruises and scratches as quickly as he normally did.
“Alright, Minotaur, what do you say we settle this giant Monster to giant Monster.” Hydra offered the Minotaur as it readied another charge.
“Don’t be an idiot. Save your strength for the big fight with Minos. He’s a weasel but he still as a Sword of Damocles to worry about.” Mel pointed out.
“Fair. Then what do you suggest?” Hydra asked.
While they talked, the Minotaur charged at them yet again. The soldier Mel rescued shrieked and fled in terror. But when the bull got close enough, Mel obliterated it with a single swing from her hammer. She was just playing when she hit him before. A serious swing from that monstrosity was a hundred times stronger than even that!
“There. Dealt with. That’s what we got this hammer for, now let’s do the same to Minos.” Mel said while walking over the chunks of her fallen foe. Hydra let out an impressed whistle before following her.
“Sure thing. Then what do you say we have beef for the victory party. I feel for it for some reason.” Hydra joked as he and Mel went to make their exit.
Back in Minos’ throne room, the nobles began panicking! These two attackers’ physical power was off the charts. Minos had never met anyone with such incredible Power! They might have been as strong as Zeus himself!
“Minos! You know better than to show fear in the face of an enemy!” a warrior said from the corner. All the noble guests turned to face him.
“Great Asterion!” Minos gave a look of relief when he saw his mentor and his father’s closest ally enter the room.
“The palace has been on high alert for too long. I knew something serious must have been at our doorsteps. These two are overwhelming with Power, the likes of which I haven’t seen since the Great Titan War.” Asterion explained while watching playback of Mel shattering the Minotaur in one blow.
“However, any random ruffian can become physically strong. That overreliance of brute force makes them arrogant and easy to triumph over. After all, there is more to battle the strength alone. That was how we defeated the Titans, and it is also how we, the Neo Kuretes shall handle our pesky intruders.” Asterion declared as his elite warriors presented themselves to the nobles.
Each one of them was more Powerful than the worthless armies Minos had sensed before and from each of them he sensed mysterious and mighty Power. Yes… Mel and Hydra were in for it now.
