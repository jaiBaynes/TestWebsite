*Myth 15.55: Talio

As Mel and Hydra continued their attack on Minos, they made their way up from the basement Labrinth to the main floor where they fell from. The palace could shift around, but so long as they kept moving in the direction of the highest source of Power they could detect, they would eventually make it to Minos. They sensed Power above, so up they went. They made their way to the stairs to ascend to the next level, speeding things up to move faster than the Palace’s pieces could shift around. However, just before the stars, Mel suddenly stopped.
“Mel, what’s up?” Hydra asked as he looked back from the top of the stairs.
“The armor of the Kuretes…” Mel explained in utter shock and despair.
On display as trophies were the old sets of armor worn by her father’s sacred warrior band the Kuretes. Defending Crete since time immemorial they weren’t just legends to Mel they were friends, idols she looked up to from childhood. Now they were dead, and their memory used as trophies by the invaders that killed them. It made her blood boil.
“You will be avenged.” Mel promised the Kuretes.
Hydra began to walk down the stairs but found he wasn’t making any progress. As he looked down the stairs were eternally shifting up like some kind of escalator. Then when he looked up again, Mel was gone!
“Mel!” her teammate cried. He couldn’t sense her anymore. With Minos’ manipulation of the building, who knew how far apart he had separated them now! He went to the top of the stairs only to find a robed man waiting for him.
“Where’s Mel!?” Hydra demanded.
“Never you mind. I’ll be sending you to meet her soon. After all, I shall be serving as your Executioner.” The Executioner laughed.
“Cool.” Hydra immediately created a sickle out of Adamant but as he stepped up to kill his attacker, a massive gash was sliced open in his own stomach! The sickle wielder doubled over, clutching his wound in shock!
“Fool! It works every time!” the Executioner laughed and laughed.
Then Hydra stood back up. Neither party knew what happened or how to respond. They just stared at each other for a bit.
“So. I’ll admit… I wasn’t expecting that.” Hydra said while feeling his freshly healed stomach.
“Likewise. Let’s try this again…” the Executioner tried to regain his composure.
Then a massive gash sliced Hydra’s arm completely off!
“There we go.” The Executioner smiled.
“Great. It’ll take me two whole seconds to reattach this.” The villain whined in feigned irritation.
The Executioner got a wry grin as Hydra said that.
Suddenly, an overwhelming pressure filled the room and Hydra felt sick. He sank to his knees! Typhon’s spawn growled as his body ached something awful.
“Wow. It seems the boss’ theory was right. That generator that was giving you such a hard time down in the labyrinth... well there it is. It doesn’t seem to agree with you though.” The Executioner grinned.
Hydra tried reattaching his arm but found it wasn’t healing on its own. He had to pour way more Power than usual to heal it. Then he had to burn even more Power to shield himself and have any modicum of comfort. No… it wasn’t worth it. The pain of staying exposed was awful, but if he kept burning that much power to stay in good health, he would tire himself out… just like his opponent wanted.
“It seems you can heal pretty well, but the question is for how long.” The Executioner pointed at him. Hydra’s jaw then exploded. It then took its sweet time regrowing from scratched as the destroyed one rotted into dust from the generator’s exposure.
“So… anything I even think of doing to you, gets done to me instead?” Hydra assessed what happened. As soon as he thought of his attack, he was injured in that exact way. Hydra took a step forward before being thrown back as if he’d punched himself in the face. Yep. The theory held up.
“You are correct, sir. Talio: that is the name of the Epiphany revealed to me from above. With this ability any harm you do to me… anything you even think of doing to me is retaliated on you.” The Executioner laughed as Hydra clutched a bloody nose.
This sucked. Every time he was hit, he lost Power, every second he baked in that accursed generator’s light he lost Power and every time he put up a barrier to not die, he lost Power! This entire contrived scenario was made to tire him out until he couldn’t even fight or recover with his regeneration. 
It was all that generator’s fault. The direct exposure was far more excruciating than being a floor above it in the Minotaur’s chamber. If he could destroy that and focus on the opponent before him…
Hydra was then sent flying as if he’d punched himself again! But how? He wasn’t even thinking about his opponent.
“I am the executioner. The crime of attempted murder against anyone… or anything will not be tolerated. You hate that generator and for plotting its demise, you will be punished.” The Executioner explained. Hydra gasped. What could he even do then? Hydra prepared to activate his flaming eyes only to be burned alive instead.
“You can probably see why I have no problem telling you about my ability. It is impossible for anyone to defeat me!” the Executioner declared confidently. But then Hydra walked out of the flames as his body fully regenerated.
“Damn… even with you frying, this is going to take a while isn’t it?” the Executioner sighed.
“No… no it isn’t.” Hydra declared as he got a devilish grin.
Similarly, Hydra cleared his mind and thought happy thoughts as he walked over to his foe. The Executioner looked nervous.
“What are you doing?” he cried.
“Thinking happy thoughts. I can’t think about what horrible things I want to do to you, or I’ll get interrupted by it happening to me.” Hydra said.
“So what? You’re surrendering?” the Executioner demanded.
“Nope. Getting in range.” Hydra grinned before raising his sickle.
“That isn’t going to work.” The Executioner sighed. Sure enough, Hydra’s head was sliced off and fell to the ground.
“Idiot. Why would he do the one thing that everyone knows even Immortals can’t heal from?” the Executioner laughed.
His chuckle was cut short when Hydra’s headless body removed his head. The decapitated Executioner fell over… dead. Hydra’s headless body bent down and reattached its own head.
“I’m not your average Immortal.” Hydra grinned before just walking out.
The watching nobles were shocked and horrified! What kind of monsters were they up against!?
