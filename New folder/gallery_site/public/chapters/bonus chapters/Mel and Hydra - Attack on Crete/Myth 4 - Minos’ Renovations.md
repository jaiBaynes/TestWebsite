*Chapter 15.53: Minos’ Renovations

“Damn it all! We screwed it all up! Now Minos is probably long gone! I’ve lost my chance!” Mel screamed furiously.
“What do you take me for!? Some kind of coward!?” Minos cried over the speaker.
Mel was shocked! Why was he still there when he found assassins on the way? Was he an idiot!?
Minos sat among his guests who all looked to him with that exact thought. He couldn’t afford to flee, he had to prove he was worthy of his throne! If he ran away, his brothers would never let him hear the end of it.
“I am the heir to the great King Minos! Like him, I’m no coward! I will stay and gladly watch you die! I shall kill you just as my father killed yours! Then I shall present your corpse to him as his homecoming present!” Minos declared.
“Oof. You want to tell him, or should I?” Hydra awkwardly asked.
“Your father, Minos Senior isn’t coming home.” Mel coldly replied. The room went silent.
“I am on a quest to cleanse all you Minoans from my country. Naturally I started from the head. Oh, and your father was a coward. He begged for his life like a bitch as I charred him- exactly like I’m going to do to you!” Mel’s wrath boiled over in a sparking blaze of Power!
This outburst was so full of divine fury that Minos’ troops could hardly remain standing in her presence. Minos could feel that awesome power all the way from his throne-room turned safehouse. As much as they peer pressured him to stay, even his guests began to consider flight from that awful monster.
“My father shall be avenged Cretin! We are not cowards! Soldiers, what do I pay you for!? Kill them!” Minos screamed in equal fury. Though he was sure to activate an extra layer of Adamant walls in this locked room to be safe.
His troops looked to their broken weapons and then to Mel’s massive one. All those extra walls did was keep them extra locked in with those creatures. Did they really have a chance fighting them in this demented cage match?
“I wouldn’t if I were you. When she gets this mad, even I can’t stop her.” Hydra warned. The soldiers’ courage wavered. They didn’t get paid enough to deal with this.
“Besides, do you really think we were running because we were scared of you? We’re trying to be nice. If we really wanted to kill you…” the son of Typhon began as he created demonic snakes that tied around each soldier’s neck and held their gaping maws open to bite.
“I could have done it a long time ago.” he spoke, and they immediately knew he told the truth.
With the snap of his fingers the snakes decayed into dust and the army troubled him no more. They dropped their weapons and cleared a path.
“Cowards and traitors! Fine!” Minos screamed as he pushed a button.
From the walls, great Adamant gems were ejected, in critical states. All the Power they contained caused them to detonate, filling the sealed room with an explosion! Minos chuckled, happy to finally use that expensive toy he had sitting unexploded. However, as the dust settled, Mel and Hydra remained standing, completely unharmed. What was more, each and every soldier was covered head to toe in the snake’s own Adamant scales, protecting them from harm too.
“How pathetic Minos! You’ve turned my house into some impotent death carnival?” Mel cried.
“At least it was toasty.” The cold blooded one chuckled as he brushed the ashes from the blast off his unharmed body.
“Let me help with the heat then!” Minos pulled another switch.
The walls revealed yet more Adamant constructs in them. This time, rather than releasing energy they all began to suck the heat right out of the air until the room was at a chilling absolute zero! Even using Power to heat up was nullified by the divine technology Minos showed off.
However, Mel stood unharmed, surrounded by lightning that turned the air to plasma and kept her warm. Hydra for his part just melted any ice that formed on his body with his flaming gaze. Soon the device burned itself out from overuse. A poor investment.
“Hey Mel, do you think you can get my back?” he with the flaming eyes asked as he turned around to show the ice patch he missed. Mel just smacked him with her Hammer again and the sheer kinetic energy melted it off.
“Are you done?” Mel demanded as she prepared to smash her way into the next room.
“Stop! You are in a holy place. Before I can let you in farther it is only fair that I… make you holey as well.” Minos chuckled before activating the next trap.
Then a third layer of weaponry revealed itself in this kill room! From the walls came automatic bow and arrows that fired off thousands of arrows from every wall! Hydra and Mel were completely unharmed. They just looked at each other.
“So, the only thing in here we need to worry about are the shifting rooms?” the junior dragon asked.
“Pretty much. And that will mostly just make it a nuisance to find and kill Minos.” Mel said as she caught an arrow that was about to fly into her mouth.
Finally, the omnidirectional hail of projectiles ceased as the machine pathetically clicked but with no ammo to fire. Now, only an awkward silence filled the room.
“Well, that was fun. We’ll be on our way to kill you now.” Hydra waved for the camera.
As he prepared to leave, he snapped his fingers and the Adamant cocoons that kept the soldiers safe crumbled into dust.
Able to move again, the troops panicked, fleeing from the monsters and banging on the walls to try and get out. Even together the army couldn’t scratch the Adamant walls containing them. Hydra and Mel ignored them, walking to the barrier to knock it down without much effort.
But right before they could bust their way out, the floor opened up and the soldiers fell, screaming into the black void. Mel and Hydra just flew.
“Should we go catch them… or?” he began before the ceiling swiftly punched them down to! The floor quickly closed behind them. This trap room couldn’t kill them, but it had pushed them down into somewhere far more dangerous…
